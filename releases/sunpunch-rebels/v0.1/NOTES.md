# SUNPUNCH v0.1 release notes

- A brand-new SUNPUNCH: this test replaces the old game, with a fresh start (old saves are not carried over).
- Fly KITE, the orange racer, through Cloud Garden level 1: Pips, Bops and the Petal Gate that opens on the music's beat.
- SunPunch: fill the meter and hit the sun button to blast every enemy on screen and clear their shots.
- Super: fill the blue shield button for 7 seconds of stronger shots.
- The Hangar: spend the gold you earn in flight on Cannons 1 and watch them fire from your plane, at Home and in flight.
- Zaid leans on the balcony at Home and cheers when you come back with a new part fitted.
- Play in Arabic or English (Settings > Language); pick your player name to start.
- There is no real-money shop and no ads in this test: parts are bought only with gold earned in flight.
