# Sadّa7 v0.16: what's new (for players)

- FIXED: one swipe moves him one lane. A swipe no longer moves him two lanes, a resting thumb no longer moves him by itself, and he no longer snaps back after you swipe. (He still moves on his own where the road does it on purpose: the moving floor plates in NEOM, and bouncing back when he runs into the side of a vehicle.)
- FIXED: the small notes that pop up no longer cover Sadّa7. Each screen shows its note in its own place, away from him, his case and the stamp; on the results screen the note waits until the stamp lands, then flies into the Journey button.
- FIXED: while he runs in a lane his body stays clear of the Charge button and inside the screen, on straights and on bends; the Charge button stays where it was.
- NEW: from Makkah on, small hazards you have to jump light up along their top edge before you reach them, each kind in its own way; the hardest ones to spot also get a rising light column with up arrows. A parked truck, crate or wall in front of it no longer hides that light. In Makkah the lights glow softly and never flash.
- IMPROVED: coins no longer sit in front of a hazard's light. They moved to nearby spots on your path; each stage has exactly as many coins as before.
- NEW: Sadّa7 celebrates when he crosses the finish line, at the end of a region, and with a longer celebration at the NEOM finish; he keeps celebrating on the results screen and on Home right after a win, with a different move from the one before (with Reduced motion on in Settings, the results screen and Home stay still). After a journey mini-game his marker hops on the map. At the quiet heritage stations (Makkah and the Hejaz Railway Station) he stays calm and checks his route instead.
- FIXED: the map no longer shows a half-built model of the first region before you have played it.

Known in this version (we are on it):
- In a jump or a slide his body can touch the Charge button for a split second (seen on one AlUla bend stage).
- At the finish line of most stages you see him from behind, so his face does not show there; at the end of a region the camera turns to his face.
- On the results screen he is shown full length with his stamped case, so his face stays small there.
- At the quiet heritage stations his finish moves are small and calm; a gentle gesture for those stations is coming.
- A new look for Sadّa7 is not in this version yet: its sleeve ends and the blue outfit's trim need a fix first; it comes in a later version.
- On Home, on short phones, the bottom card can still hide part of his case and its stamps.
- His run, landing and backpack strap stay as they are.
- On wide phones a few small hazards (mostly in NEOM) still look small from far away; they light up before you reach them.
