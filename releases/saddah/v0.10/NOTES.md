# SADDAH v0.10: what changed

**Please update:** an earlier copy of v0.10 reached testers this morning without its download pack and stayed on the download screen. This copy replaces it; if you installed v0.10 earlier today, update to this one.

- NEW: much faster and busier driving. All 54 stages were re-tuned, so there is always something to do, and the later worlds ask more of you than the early ones.
- NEW: Hood Hop. On most stages you can hop onto the roofs of cars waiting in a queue and run along them, with a Surge of speed if you keep it going. Miss and the road bites back. It is new and still being improved.
- NEW: the red-light roof dash. On four stages the traffic stops at a red light and you can run straight along the roofs of the waiting cars to the green. It is new and still being improved.
- NEW: big moments in every world, now detailed 3D at their true size: a dust wall rolling in, a swinging crane, a car that flashes past, a giant wide load, dust devils, a pod train of lights, a truck on the horizon, a Dhub lizard popping out and a falcon swooping by. They are smooth 3D now and arrive when the road gives you a quiet moment.
- IMPROVED: the trips between cities are rebuilt as new games: call the herd and sing to the camels, race the bus to finish the bridge, leap up the cliff on the ranger's rope, toss orders from your car at the rest stop, pull focus for the cable car photos, catch the harvest, and clear the old railway line. The flight to AlUla now has gifts to toss to the desert camps and a bigger landing. They are new and still being improved.
- NEW: real, detailed 3D vehicles: all 25 kinds of cars, taxis, buses, trucks and work machines are new models, each in its natural colours (taxis, school buses and work trucks keep theirs), coming toward you on the road. At the port you can run right under a giant straddle carrier.
- NEW: each world has its own obstacles, and the scenery at the roadside now matches the real place: mud houses in the old Najdi towns, kerbs and grandstands at the circuit, real nature at the NEOM stages.
- NEW: far horizons painted for many more places, so the skyline behind you looks like where you are, plus a postcard for every world on the loading and map screens.
- NEW: the friendly animals by the road (the dhub, the hoopoe, the goat, the baboon, the rock hyraxes and the falcon) really move now, and they show up on your side of the road so you can see them. The baboon even hops onto a parked car.
- NEW: a twist on the car-roof hops: in six levels a beam hangs just behind the last bus. Hop off on the beat and you sail over it, or slide under it.
- IMPROVED: phones with less memory load lighter copies of the biggest pictures, so the game keeps running; every other phone keeps full quality.
- NEW: the golden riyal has a new look: a thick, shiny gold coin you can spot while it spins.
- NEW: results, loading, briefing and trip screens now look like one family, and your keepsakes hang on Sadّa7's case and backpack.
- NEW: the 3D model of the first city on the map, and a download pack: the biggest art downloads once when you first play (with progress, and it carries on if it stops), so the game keeps full quality.

Not in this version: a new look for Sadّa7 himself (he stays as in the last version). Smoothness on low-end phones (Galaxy A16 class) was not measured this version.
