# Sadّa7 v0.11: what's new (for players)

- NEW: Sadّa7 has a new, more lifelike look: a new face and outfit, and new slides that go left, right or straight, each with its own way of getting back up.
- NEW: the new Sadّa7 also climbs in the rope trip game and flies the plane in the plane ride, where the falcon lands on his gloved arm. The two shop outfits now show on his new look too.
- COMING NEXT: the menu pictures still show his earlier look for now. They get the new look in a coming update.
- FIXED: some obstacles (landing birds, boxes on the raised roads, rising barriers) could be invisible or drawn in the wrong place and still hit you. Now every obstacle that can hit you is always drawn where it really is.
- FIXED: the game could close by itself a few seconds into the rope trip game (on the way from world 3 to world 4), when its music started over for the first time.
- IMPROVED: smoother running. Each phone now picks one steady speed it can always keep (60 frames a second on fast phones, 30 on smaller ones), so the road no longer stutters on phones with fast screens. The picture quality is the same as before.
- IMPROVED: no more freeze at the start of a stage. Everything a stage needs is prepared behind the loading card, with a progress bar, so the first seconds of the run stay smooth.
- IMPROVED: no more small jump every few seconds while you run: your progress is now saved in the background.
- IMPROVED: retrying a stage is instant, and the game no longer grows heavier the longer you play.
- IMPROVED: the download screen shows how many MB are done, how many are left and about how long it will take, even while it is still connecting. If the connection drops or the server is busy, it waits and carries on from where it stopped.
- NEW: your player account. At first open a short welcome step asks your age, lets older players sign in with Google Play Games or Game Center, and lets you pick a player name from a list. Tap "Later" to keep playing as a guest at any time. Your progress is saved to your account, so you can keep it when you move to another phone.
- IMPROVED: the coins have one name in each language on every screen: "coins" in English, العملات in Arabic.
