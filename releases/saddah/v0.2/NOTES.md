# SADDAH v0.2 — what's new

The first playable SADDAH: the whole journey from the Najd desert to the NEOM coast.

- Young Saddah in 3D carries the one sealed Amanah of Tomorrow through nine worlds: Desert, Riyadh, Qiddiya, Tuwaiq, Taif, Makkah, Jeddah, AlUla and NEOM.
- 54 short stages, each checked to be fair: every jump and slide leaves at least about half a second to react, also with Assist timing on.
- Swipe or on-screen buttons; a gentle first-time lesson for jump and slide.
- Each world has its own look, music, ambience and a finale set-piece with its own finish arch.
- Story: Grandfather's car stops in the sand, he hands Saddah the case; locals wave him on at every world; the case is handed over at the ceremony on the NEOM coast.
- Collect Qoroosh, buy small upgrades (falcon magnet, shemagh shield, boost and more) and two outfits; never needed to finish.
- Arabic and English, right-to-left, text up to 150 %, reduced motion, assist timing, separate volumes.
- Plays offline; no ads or purchases switched on.

Still to come: real-phone performance tuning, a native Saudi listen to the voices and dialect, and deeper world detail.
