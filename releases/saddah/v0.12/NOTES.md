# Sadّa7 v0.12: what's new (for players)

- NEW: smoother movement. The camera follows Sadّa7 more softly, with a small dip when he lands, and his moves blend into each other more cleanly.
- NEW: a livelier menu. Sadّa7 now stands on the Home screen in his new look, strikes a pose from your last run, and the stamps on his case show what you have done.
- NEW: every button in the game has one new bevelled look, with a click sound and a light buzz when you press it.
- NEW: try outfits on in the shop. Sadّa7 turns to show the outfit before you buy it, the next thing you can afford comes first, and each price you cannot reach yet says how many coins you still need.
- NEW: the menu pictures and the 18 story pictures now show Sadّa7's new look (the comic beats inside the rope trip still show his earlier drawing; next version).
- NEW: the Coastal and Desert Stitch outfits now look like their shop pictures: a pale sea-blue thobe with a teal backpack, and a sand thobe with red diamond embroidery.
- NEW: all nine worlds have finished obstacles and roadsides in their own colours, so each world looks like its own place. Where obstacles are and how they hit you did not change.
- IMPROVED: tapping Play opens the stage faster (about a third less waiting than v0.11 in our tests): the loading card grows straight out of the Play button.
- IMPROVED: the first-time lessons now match the controls you use, and the jump lesson only passes when you really jump.
- IMPROVED: stage goals show on the map and in the briefing, and tapping a goal tells you exactly what it asks for.
- IMPROVED: your account now starts with your player name: the name you enter creates it. Deleting your account can be undone for 14 days.
- IMPROVED: the content download says so plainly when it cannot read how much space your phone has, and carries on safely from where it stopped.

Known in this version (we are on it):
- The first content download is about 170 MB bigger than before (about 1.6 GB: lighter copies for phones with less memory).
- Sadّa7's feet can slide a little while he runs; smoother footwork comes in the next version.
- On the Desert Stitch outfit the chest embroidery is a thin line, not the wide band of its shop picture.
- The slide dust in the Najd Desert and NEOM was improved like the other worlds but not re-checked there yet.
- At the very first stage, the place name is hidden for a moment while the first lesson hint is on screen.
