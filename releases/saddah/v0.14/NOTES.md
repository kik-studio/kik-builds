# Sadّa7 v0.14: what's new (for players)

- NEW: a wider view of the road on every phone: you see more of the side lanes and what is coming, and Sadّa7 and the obstacles look a little smaller.
- NEW: the Desert Stitch outfit has its tall stand collar with the row of diamonds, as in its shop picture.
- IMPROVED: Home shows a clean sky picture behind Sadّa7 (no blurred patch), on phones and tablets.
- FIXED: his backpack straps no longer end in a loose tip under his arm.
- FIXED: the luggage trolleys at AlUla Airport and the crossing at Wadi Hanifa now start moving inside the picture, with the same timing on the road.
- FIXED: crossing obstacles no longer stand in a lane for a moment and then jump to the side when they appear.
- FIXED: the results stamp now sits on his case without hiding it, at the end of a stage and on Home.
- IMPROVED: the sound never peaks too loud.

Known in this version (we are on it):
- Sadّa7's feet can still slide a little while he runs, mostly when he lands; a new run and landing for his speed is still being made.
- A few moving boxes on the AlUla bends, and on most phones one standing box on the first stage, come into view a little late (the moving ones are in view for most of their move).
- From some angles his backpack strap still shows a small hook or twist.
- The first content download stays about 170 MB bigger than in v0.11 (lighter copies for phones with less memory, nothing cut).
