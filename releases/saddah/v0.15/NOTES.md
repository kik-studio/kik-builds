# Sadّa7 v0.15: what's new (for players)

- NEW: at the hand-over Sadّa7 celebrates, with a different move each time (never the same one twice in a row), and the camera turns for a moment so you see his face. At the quiet heritage stations (Makkah and the Hejaz Railway Station) he stays calm and checks his route instead.
- IMPROVED: on bends the view turns a little into the bend early, so the boxes on the AlUla bends come into view in time.
- IMPROVED: obstacles are easier to read in the wide view: the ones on the road cast their own shadow, barriers and overhead beams carry reflective tape, and falling rocks keep their warning ring.
- IMPROVED: the results stamp shows the city name in a size you can read, on a tag beside his case.
- IMPROVED: the map scrolls and drags more smoothly.
- FIXED: the map keeps working if one of its pictures is slow to load.

Known in this version (we are on it):
- His run and landing stay as they are (his feet can still slide a little, mostly when he lands), and the backpack strap can still show a small hook from some angles.
- On Home, on short phones, the bottom card can hide part of his case and its stamps.
- On wide phones a few low barriers still look small in the wide view, and one box on an AlUla bend can still come into view a little late.
- The message on the results screen can cover his head for a moment.
- In the left lane his legs can sit behind the Charge button.
- The first seconds after the map opens can still feel a little uneven.
