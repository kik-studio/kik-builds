# VEIL v0.10 release notes

- Ranking: a compact rank and best at the top that stays while the list scrolls, cleaner rows with the top three marked, one clear status line, and a soft open and close.
- Home and results: your best and your place read as one achievement; the results screen shows how far you are from your best.
- First open: your name comes first and signing in is clearly optional; the Home buttons stay hidden until you are done.
- Settings: every row looks the same, with the one Music slider kept.
- Flight: a shorter launch, the first turn comes sooner and the first climb or dive comes about twice as early, and a crash flashes where you really hit. Results come up at once.
- Look: softer pearls with less glow, the aircraft stands out from bright arches, calmer glass, deeper tunnels, less white glare from bright walls close to the camera, thinner trails.
- Music: pearl phrases follow the chords and are never dropped when the music is busy; Pause silences every part of the music; Retry never hears the last run's ending; the music answers Fly at once; lightning and its musical hit land together, with a lighter dip.
- There are no ads in this test version, and nothing to buy.
- This test version is for Android; the iPhone build follows after Nov 1.
