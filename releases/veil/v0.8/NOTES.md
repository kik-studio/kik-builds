# VEIL v0.8 release notes

- Only music now: no wind, storm or tap sounds. The one Music slider controls every sound (all the way down = silence).
- The music plays with your flight: thunder rolls in on the beat, close passes and pearls ring in tune, a run of pearls resolves at the next gate, and speed and height lift the score.
- A new loading page: the VEIL sky, the storm and your aircraft move while the world is built, with a bar that only goes forward.
- Pick your name and one tap: Pilot (he) or Pilot (she); the Arabic text speaks to you the right way. Change it any time in Settings.
- Settings > Data reads more evenly, and the Arabic ranking card shows your place clearly.
- There are no ads in this test version, and nothing to buy.
- This test version is for Android; the iPhone build follows after Nov 1.
