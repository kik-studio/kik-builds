# VEIL 1.0 release notes

- This is VEIL 1.0: the same game as 0.15, now numbered 1.0. This is the version that goes to Google Play and the App Store.
- Nothing in the game changed since 0.15: the flights, the courses, the screens and the ranking are the same as in 0.15.
- This test copy still has no ads and nothing to buy. The store copy shows an ad after every second finished flight and offers Remove Ads, a one-time purchase.

Not finished yet (10):
- Smooth play and the first open on phones: the 0.15 changes (the same in 1.0) are not measured on phones yet. The last phone numbers (an earlier copy of 0.14): slowest frames on a Galaxy A54 63.4 ms (aim: 50 ms), first open 43.9 seconds; a high-end Google phone 33.3 ms (aim: 25 ms) and 29.7 seconds (aim: 10 seconds).
- Touch delay on phones: the faster touch from 0.15 is not measured on a phone yet (last Galaxy A54 run: 166 ms from touch to screen, aim: 40 ms).
- A full real-phone check of the phone keyboard, cancelling sign-in, offline scores, larger text and WhatsApp sharing has not run yet.
- A 15-minute run on real phones (Overdrive and the four places: smoothness and memory) has not run yet; the same 15-minute run passed on a test phone on our computers.
- The music and sound of the whole game, the Overdrive music included, get one more listening pass.
- TalkBack cannot read the flight buttons on Android yet: the game engine has no screen-reader support there. The camera and Overdrive buttons already carry their names in English and Arabic for when it does.
- App protection for the ranking is not on yet. This test copy works without it.
- The ranking's health alert is not set up yet. Nothing in the app changes for it.
- From the High view, when a moving shutter breaks apart as it should, its pieces can still look like thin slivers; the small cloud islands on the horizon can look like flat discs at phone size, and one pillar edge can show a thin seam.
- iPhone: the iPhone copy comes through TestFlight; we have not yet seen it open on a real iPhone ourselves. Tell us if it opens and plays.
