# VEIL v0.11 release notes

- New course: the first minute asks for more turns, climbs and dives, and a new moving gate, the Slide Shutter, opens and closes as you fly at it.
- New ranking season: the course changed, so the ranking starts again. Your old best stays visible as your best on the previous course.
- Ranking: ties share the same place everywhere, a better run always replaces your old one, and the results line says plainly whether your run was saved, is still being sent, or is being checked.
- Ranking safety: you can report a name or hide a player from a ranking row, names are filtered, and the name step says that by continuing you agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy.
- New intro: the pearl aircraft is lit from the first frame, then flies in to Home.
- Pearls: a one-time hint says pearls charge the Veil; it comes once you have learned to steer, in calm air.
- Veil button: clearer ready state and short wording; a save rewind now reads as a save.
- Crash: the aircraft stays where it hit with a small mark at the contact point while results come up; Retry is instant.
- Results: distance first, then your best, then Retry; how far you are from your best is shown only when you are close to it.
- Ranking screen: a clean header with your place and best, taller rows with easy-to-read distances, the top three marked, your own row marked by more than colour.
- First open: the name card should move up with the keyboard, Done should send once, and your typed name should be kept if you leave the app or cancel a sign-in (please try it).
- Look: far fewer sky streaks when you tilt, a softer haze on Home, no dark disc around the aircraft, obstacles that read as one object, and less glare from bright walls close up.
- Music: every pearl gets a musical answer even when the music is busy, and Music at 0 stays silent.
- Large text and screen readers: bigger text sizes fit on every screen we checked, and the camera and Veil buttons were fixed for TalkBack (please try it).
- Settings: links to the terms, refund policy and privacy page; if you asked to delete your account you can cancel within 14 days.
- There are no ads in this test version, and nothing to buy.
- This test version is for Android; the iPhone build follows after Nov 1.

Not in this version yet:
- The course is at least twice as demanding only in the first minute; after that it is about the same as the last version. The next version works on the rest of the run.
- Some glare is still there on bright walls close up, most of all in the High view.
- In the Dawn sky with the High view, the aircraft still stands out less than we want.
- With Reduced Motion on, the intro has one jump.
- The music's shaping across the whole flight is still open; listening on phone speakers is for you to tell us.
- Opening the game is still slower than two versions ago; this version cuts part of the start-up work, and the phone numbers come with this build.
- Smoothness has not been measured on real phones for this version yet.
- The ground in flight is unchanged; making it as rich as the sky is next.
- Telling solid glass from pass-through panes at a distance is still being worked on.
- The Google Play Games leaderboard for the new course is not linked yet; the in-game ranking works.
- The ranking server's health alert and backup drill are still being set up by the studio.
