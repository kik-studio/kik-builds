# VEIL v0.13 release notes

- This version starts a fresh test account: enter your name once more.
- Fewer empty stretches in a run: after the first minute, no gap between required moves is longer than 4 seconds on any of the 8 test runs (version 0.12 had 5 longer gaps, the longest about 7 seconds).
- Moves are more connected later in a run: where one move used to end in a long empty stretch, the next move now follows, and a short pause stays after the big turns (at least 1.79 seconds to recover on every test run).
- After the Eye of the storm the flight no longer ends in a quiet tube: the way out now holds a short stretch of its own.
- Pearls: on the way out of the Eye of the storm a few pearls sit off the safe line again, so catching them takes a small risk, and one easy pearl in a glass tube is gone.
- The curved tunnels now leave the same gap whenever you reach them, so their timing no longer depends on luck (never wider than in 0.12).
- Later in a run the course links moves more tightly. Averaged over the 8 test runs, each part of the course after the first 3 minutes asks at least as many moves as in 0.12 (between 1.6 and 5.6 percent more); on the ranked course everyone flies, every one of those parts asks more than in 0.12.
- The Crystal Canyon's right wall now shows as much facet detail as the left wall (the same detail within 10 percent), the Cloud Sea horizon has fewer flat pale areas (in the High view it can still look like a row of pale panels; that is being fixed), the storm coil has more detail on its face, and the storm floor shows more depth.
- During Overdrive the storm now gives way ahead of you: the clouds and violet streaks of light at the edges of the route part outward in a travelling V that opens in front of the aircraft, for the whole 10 seconds, on both the Close and the High view.
- The bubble around the aircraft is easier to read at the end: in the last 2 seconds it shrinks onto the aircraft in two steps on the beat, so you can see the time running out without looking at the rim.
- The camera no longer slips inside a pane of glass: glass between the camera and the aircraft fades out as it gets close, so you never see the back of a pane.
- The ranking starts fresh for this version on the new test servers. Your 0.12 best still shows on Home as the earlier course, but your 0.12 runs are not shown in this test version's ranking.
- On first open, Home now shows the same lines in English and in Arabic: your best and the earlier course.
- There are no ads in this test version, and nothing to buy.
- This test version is for Android; the iPhone build follows.
