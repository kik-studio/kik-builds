# VEIL v0.14 release notes

- The ranking starts fresh with this version: flights from 0.13 are not kept. The Earlier course tab shows only flights sent from phones still on 0.13, and while it is empty it says so plainly.
- Pearls: a few more pearls now sit just off the safe line on the spiral of gates into the Eye of the storm, in the moving openings and between the staggered pillars, so catching them takes a small risk. 38.2 percent of pearls sit on the safe line (41.7 percent in 0.13).
- The Settings sheet is now light, a see-through glass panel over VEIL's own sky, like Home, Results and the ranking.
- More of the loading now happens while Home is already on screen (the later places and the first-flight effects), so Fly can take a moment to become ready after Home appears. The open time on phones is still being measured (see below).
- Overdrive breaks from the High view: the glass shards are bigger, with a darker edge in their own glass's colour, so they stand out against the sky and a break reads from the High view too.
- Cloud Sea horizon: the row of pale panels along the horizon is now a band of dark cloud banks, tinted by the mood of the sky (storm, dawn, twilight).
- There are no ads in this test version, and nothing to buy.

Not finished yet (12):
- Smooth play on mid phones: on one short run of an earlier test copy of this version, the slowest frames on a Galaxy A54 took 63.4 ms (aim: 50 ms; the sent 0.13 in another test run on the same phone: 66.0 ms). A high-end Google phone ran 0.13 at 33.3 ms (aim: 25 ms). Longer runs on this exact version follow.
- Touch delay: one run on a Galaxy A54 measured 166 ms from touch to screen (aim: 40 ms). The slow frames of that run explain it; the touch path itself is not slower than in 0.13.
- First open: on a Galaxy A54 an earlier test copy of this version took 43.9 seconds to open the first time (the sent 0.13 in another test run on the same phone: 45.2 seconds) and 13.9 seconds the second time. A high-end Google phone opened 0.13 in 29.7 seconds (aim: 10 seconds). The latest loading changes are not measured on phones yet.
- TalkBack cannot read the flight buttons on Android yet: the game engine has no screen-reader support there. The camera and Overdrive buttons already carry their names in English and Arabic for when it does.
- A 15-minute run on real phones (Overdrive and the four places: smoothness and memory) is not done yet, nor a full real-phone check of the phone keyboard, cancelling sign-in, offline scores, larger text and WhatsApp sharing.
- The music and sound of the whole game, the Overdrive music included, get one more listening pass before the store version.
- Store-version pieces are still in progress: ads and the Remove Ads purchase, the privacy page, app protection, and the ranking's health alert and backup test.
- From the High view the turning fins and the slalom walls can be harder to read than from the Close view (the same as in 0.13).
- Pearls: 38.2 percent still sit on the safe line; the aim was 37.9 percent.
- iPhone: the iPhone copy comes through TestFlight only when its iPhone build passes its check. If it does not, the versions page may still show an iPhone link next to the Android download.
- From the High view the moving shutters still break into thin slivers, and the small cloud islands on the horizon can look like flat discs at phone size.
- With the text size at Largest, some buttons are smaller than a comfortable finger tap: the ranking tabs, the Settings rows, the name field, the pilot choices and the Google sign-in on the first screen, and the Settings button. All of them can still be seen and pressed.
