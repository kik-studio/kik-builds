Nova Strike v0.9: your aircraft are the collection ships, the whole game restyled, one Journey map.

Your aircraft
- Striker, Guardian and Arcanist are now the ships from the aircraft collection: gold, chrome and gems. Every Power level bolts real parts onto the ship and escort drones lock in beside it; the Super shows the Super form.
- Each aircraft has the collection's four premium finishes (names in English and Arabic). Old skins carry over by look; Supporters keep their gold finish; nobody loses a skin.

Upgrades
- Three clear paths: Weapons, Hull & Shield, Special Systems. Each card shows what the next buy gives you. Every few buys your ship visibly changes (new abilities like Overcharge, Deflect, Last Light, Crown).
- Your old upgrades are moved over automatically; no credits are lost.

Enemies and attacks
- Enemies are polished chrome and obsidian with ruby cores; bosses restyled part by part, with new intro and death scenes.
- Lasers warn, grow, burn exactly where they are drawn, and fade. Shots always leave a gun or eye on the enemy's body. Every wave is checked so there is always a way out.
- Obstacles and walls match their sector; their edges hit exactly where they are drawn. Six obstacles can be shot apart.

Campaign
- One tall Journey map up through the six sectors. It opens where you are; tap a place to see the mission and PLAY. Beating a sector's boss lifts the haze over it.

Menus and sound
- Simpler menus: one clear thing to do on every screen; Arabic checked on every screen.
- Fuller, comfortable sound: crisp rapid fire, weighty Supers, warnings you always hear above the music.
