# Nova Strike v0.22

- Your ship keeps the same body at every power level and in Super; power-ups now add separate side pods beside it (one pair, then two), sliding in as you power up.
- The Solar laser now reaches the top of the screen and pulses on and off, from power level 3 and in Super.
- Super no longer makes your ship bigger or holds it in the middle: you steer freely the whole time.
- Ordinary enemies fly closer to you, each kind at its own distance; bosses and elite fights play as before.
- A cleaner look on every menu: open screens, one main button, the ship bright in the Hangar.
- Track one upgrade goal; buying an upgrade plays a short install moment, and after a mission you can upgrade and fly the next one straight away.
- Your call sign now creates your account at first open (it works offline too), and Delete account now stays fully quiet until you start again.
- Fixed: the pilot buttons in Settings now show their names instead of code text, and the Arabic download bar now fills from the right.
