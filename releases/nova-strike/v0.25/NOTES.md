# Nova Strike v0.25

## Google Play

Smoother Void weapons, same look; a Void bomb on a boss no longer stutters. Shorter pause when a mission starts. Enemy fire keeps one pace at any frame rate. Bigger buttons across the menus. Arc power bolts are easy to follow. After a hit, a shield flash and ring show your power is safe for a few seconds. Enemy shots stay visible through the Solar lance. Sector 2-4 bosses no longer fall too fast; Sector 5 Lancer waves spaced out, 5.06's doors close tighter. Sector 4-6 crossers take their shot.

## All channels

- Smoother Void weapons: the Void effects (the Torpedoes' spiral included) now take about a quarter to a third less drawing work at Power 2 to 4 and in the Super, with the same look.
- A Void bomb that hits a big boss no longer makes the game stutter for a moment.
- Shorter pause when a mission starts: the fight is now prepared while the Briefing is on screen, so the pause when play begins is much shorter (on our test computer the worst start frame went from half a second or more to under 0.2 s; not yet measured on phones).
- Enemies fire at about the same pace whether your phone runs at 30, 60 or 120 frames per second.
- Bigger buttons across the menus: Settings, Account, Welcome, pause, defeat and results screens, the Store and the first-flight Skip have larger buttons and switches that are easier to hit; screen titles stay centred.
- The Arc power shot's bolts are now easy to follow: each bolt has a bright head, and its path stays on screen a moment longer before it fades into the target. Damage and timing are unchanged.
- New shield flash and ring after a hit: when you are hit, a flash and a thin ring around your ship show a short guard moment (about 5 seconds). The hit that opens it can still cost power, but further hits during the guard never do.
- Enemy shots stay readable through the Solar lance: each enemy shot inside the lance now has a dark edge, also with High contrast on.
- The Sector 2, 3 and 4 bosses (Furnace Sovereign, Storm Seraph and Null Oracle) keep one of their phases a little longer when your ship beats them very fast, so the whole fight plays out. Their health is unchanged.
- Sector 5: the Lancer waves in missions 5.02 and 5.04 wait for a quieter moment and come with wider gaps, and the hangar doors in 5.06 close tighter again.
- Sectors 4 to 6: enemies that fly across the screen now get their shot in before they leave, unless firing at that moment would leave you no way to dodge.

Not finished yet:
- The first Super of a mission can still hitch once on some phones.
- The very first launch has one slow frame while the game prepares, and the shorter pause at mission start is measured on our test computer only, not yet on phones.
- Mission 2.05 can be harder with the Arcanist than in earlier versions; we are still checking it.
