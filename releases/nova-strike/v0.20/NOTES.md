# Nova Strike v0.20

- Bosses react to your shots: your fire lights the boss where it hits (orange, purple or blue by weapon).
- Broken boss parts really tear off the model and tumble away, leaving a glowing scar.
- At a phase change each boss turns in 3D and shows a glowing weak spot that takes extra damage.
- Every boss has its own big moment: Algol's eye opens, the Furnace slams its vents open.
- New Boss Hall (on Home and in the Hangar): every boss you have beaten turns there as a 3D trophy.
- Bosses you beat from now on also show the day you beat them.
- The mission briefing now shows the boss as a turning 3D model.
- Smoother boss arrivals: most bosses now appear without a stutter; a few still hitch briefly.
- Smoothness in the big boss fights on mid-range phones (Galaxy A54 class) is still being improved.
