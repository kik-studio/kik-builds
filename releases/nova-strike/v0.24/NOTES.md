# Nova Strike v0.24

- A bolder, richer look: deeper colours instead of the pale finish, finished metal on every ship, and each pilot, ship and class shares one colour identity.
- Arc and Void weapons rebuilt at every power level: charged Arc bolts, crescents, relays near the enemies and a Storm Weave with rhythm; heavy Void slugs, corkscrew torpedoes, a Black Star you can see hold its force, and a new Eclipse Collapse Super. Solar keeps its mixed arsenal, with a stronger lance.
- Shots start from the real guns on your ship, and the ship stays one solid piece with its modules.
- Enemies fly in clear lead, answer and release patterns; their lasers sweep smoothly and fairly, and bosses show a real weak point and a proper finish. ALGOL's twins are rebuilt.
- Power-ups show each milestone, losing power is easier to see and recover from, and pickups flow smoothly to your ship.
- New Home, Briefing, Campaign, Hangar and Modes screens: one clear next flight, one upgrade at a time, and you see the part lock in when you install it.
- Victory and defeat screens are shorter and clearer, and a mission clear now pays out at once.
- In a fight, warnings and info sit at the screen edges as small tags; no card covers the middle any more. The scenery steps back from your ship.
- Settings and the other menus are restyled with clearer type and Arabic spacing; a new Pilot voice slider sets the pilots' voice on its own.
- Texts: the Striker's side weapon is called Escort Fire everywhere, and after deleting your account the start-again screen asks for a new name (no more "Play offline for now").
- Endless has new curated sector waves with almost no empty stretches.
- Smoother play: busy Arc and Void fights draw much less, and short loading and hitch fixes.
- New weapon sounds for each class, a cleaner mix, music that leaves room for the fight, and lighter, more purposeful vibration.
- Your ship can now be hit across most of its body, not just a small centre dot; enemies attack more (almost every enemy fires at least once), some hits can't be dodged, and burst shots leave with a gap between them. Each hit costs a little less hull, so missions stay winnable.
