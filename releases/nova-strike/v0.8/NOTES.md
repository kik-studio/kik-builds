Nova Strike v0.8: the Super is back, weapons you can see, smoother play.

Super
- The Super stops time again: about 1.7 s of deep slow motion, the pilot slides in at the top of the screen, says their line, and the aircraft transforms. Nothing can hit you while the pilot is on screen, and the middle of the screen (where threats are) stays clear.
- Supers come more often: about 3-4 per mission.

Weapons
- Every Power level adds one new weapon you can see. Striker: twin needles, smoke-trail rockets, crossing slipstream darts, a flame lance. Guardian: black marbles, swirl wells, stacking facets, a black star. Arcanist: zigzag hops, mark sigils, a lightning fence, a branching lance.
- Shots are small and crisp again; no giant yellow column.

Look
- The first enemies glow crimson and mean again, with clean edges.
- The fight screen is brighter: lantern lights along the road, a key light on your aircraft. (Measured: lightness 26.9, up from 20.3, target 26.5 met; the dark part of the screen is 55 %, down from 64 % but above the 50 % aim, accepted by the studio lead for this version.)
- VICTORY is a reward again: a big pilot portrait, a brighter screen.

Pilots
- Each pilot now has many lines per moment (up to 10, 18 for bosses), in English and Saudi Arabic. You won't hear the same line twice in a mission; only the Super line repeats.

Challenge
- Later sectors bite again if you stop dodging; bosses tuned so every aircraft can win with the upgrades you own by then.

Smoother play
- Everything a mission needs is loaded before it starts, and effects are prepared on the loading screen, so missions no longer stutter when a new effect appears. The first launch after installing prepares your aircraft once (about 50 s, during the HD download); in the first missions after installing, 2-3 short stutters per mission can remain (worst about a quarter of a second, measured on a Pixel 10 Pro XL), and none after that (0 on the second launch).
- The HD art downloads at start-up, before you play. On the web it is saved in your browser, so it doesn't download again on your next visit (tested in Chrome and Edge; Firefox and Android Chrome not tested yet). The browser may still clear it if your device runs short of space; then it downloads once more.
