# Nova Strike v0.15: what changed

- The game now opens only with the full HD graphics: on first launch (and after an update that brings new graphics) it downloads them first, then starts. No more playing with lighter graphics in the meantime.
- The download screen shows the progress, carries on where it stopped after a dropped connection, and says clearly when you need to connect to the internet to finish.
- A damaged download is fetched again by itself and never used.

Honest note on the art:
- 1 finished look item(s) without a passing look check yet
- 5100 art files still use the earlier look without a passing look check
- the game's look settings are not locked yet
- parts of the look are still below our own 4.5 bar; it keeps improving in the next versions
