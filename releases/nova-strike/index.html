Nova Strike v0.3: sharper look, livelier combat, simpler menus.

Look
- Sharper on high-end phones: the aircraft render at full resolution, with warm key light on the gold trim and cool edges on the graphite.
- Tighter glow, crisper shots with thin dark outlines, calmer highlights behind the fight.
- Enemies are now dark burgundy and gunmetal with crimson lights; only you and your rewards are gold.
- Full-quality cutscenes, backgrounds and portraits download in the background after the first launch (Settings shows the progress).

Combat
- Three enemy attack styles: scouts fire short aimed bursts, assault ships strafe with fan volleys, heavies charge a narrow beam, then pause.
- Enemy fire has new shapes: needles, teardrops, heavy bolts and beams, in coral and raspberry.
- Obstacles: tumbling wreckage and station gates, always announced and always with a way through.
- Solar Breaker replaces Overdrive Dash: a crescent of solar fire that cuts through enemies.
- Arc Relay now strikes the enemies and chains between them.
- Guardian's shield absorbs hits, then releases a shockwave.
- Stronger Super transformations with their own sounds.
- New pickup icons, each with its own sound.

Menus
- Home has three buttons: PLAY, UPGRADE and MORE.
- Installing an upgrade shows the part being built onto your aircraft.
- Upgrade tiers open as you clear the campaign (tier II after Stage 1, III after Stage 3, IV after Stage 5). Tiers you already own stay yours.
- Bigger, bolder text everywhere, in English and Arabic.
- First flight: a short, safe opening that teaches steering, pickups and your special.

Nova Strike v0.2: the big look pass.

New
- Cinematics: an intro for every stage, an entrance and a death scene for all 6 bosses, a ship-assembly scene the first time you open each craft in the Hangar, and living pilot portraits. All can be skipped with a tap.
- Music and voice: a theme for every boss, a Hangar theme, victory and defeat stings, and 72 pilot voice lines in English and Saudi Arabic.
- Campaign is now an illustrated sector map; Mastery, Settings, Credits and Endless have painted backgrounds; the game opens on the key-art splash.

Better
- All 13 enemies redrawn, with visible battle damage as they weaken.
- Dominion Prime is now the crown colossus; Furnace Sovereign and Null Oracle redrawn; every boss cracks open between phases.
- Enemy fire glows with a dark outline and never looks like your shots; explosions come in 4 sizes; each weapon has its own hits.
- New Lantern Road, Emberwake, Tempest and Crownfall backdrops, each with its own light; wide screens no longer repeat the background.
- Your craft looks the same in combat as in the Hangar (black and gold), and installed upgrades show on it. Guardian and Arcanist got new art and a true "mastered" look.
- Marcus and Lyra have new faces; Veyron's victory smile is calm.
- New Pause and Training screens.

Fixed
- Arabic: no more violet box over the play area; numbers, signs and Latin names read correctly.
- Rotating or resizing no longer pauses the game.
- On tablets and computers the background around the play area is one continuous painting (no hard edges).

Gameplay
- You now reach full weapon power about halfway through a mission: each power core raises your level by one, and there is one more guaranteed core early on.

Known
- The download is bigger (166 MB) because of the cinematics.
