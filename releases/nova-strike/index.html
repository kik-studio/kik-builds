▶ Play in browser · ⬇ Android APK · iPhone: TestFlight

Nova Strike v0.6: unique bosses, a real difficulty wall, arcade warnings, a true Super moment, health you can feel.

Bosses: each one has its own mechanic now
- Algol (Lantern Road): twin engines orbiting each other. Only the twin in the light can be hurt; every eclipse swaps them. Enrage: total eclipse.
- Furnace Sovereign (Emberwake): forges a giant hammer during the fight. Break the glowing rivets before it's done, or it slams the half of the screen you're in. Enrage: meltdown, a hammer every few seconds.
- Storm Seraph (Tempest): marks you as its lightning rod. Fly next to its own drones and the lightning hits them instead ("REDIRECTED!"). Enrage: the whole sky strikes.
- Null Oracle (Hollow Veil): a sweeping vision cone. Outside it you're safe but can't hurt it; inside you can, but it fires straight at you. Later: two cones and a false one. Enrage: the eye opens wide and closes on you.
- Regent's Spear (Crownfall): locks on over 3 seconds. Hide behind its own wreckage and drones to break the lock and open its weak point. Enrage: the whole fleet locks on.
- Dominion Prime (Final Dominion): THE HARVEST steals your rockets, then shells, then laser; break its crown vessels to take them back, then finish it with your full arsenal.
- Every phase is faster than the last, late phases throw two attacks at once, and every enrage is a real wall.

Much harder, and upgrades matter
- Enemy damage now grows every sector (up to 3.5x in the last sector), bosses hit harder, and the long safety blink after a hit is gone.
- Without Hangar upgrades you can't beat the later bosses; upgraded and dodging, you can. Defense upgrades are the answer to damage.
- Story mode stays gentler; Veteran is harder.

Arcade warnings
- Big full-width warning bands with hazard stripes, huge titles, a siren and flashing screen edges before every obstacle and boss attack, in English and Arabic, each sector with its own style. After the first two times, a quick icon at the danger spot instead.

The Super moment
- Pressing Super slows time, your pilot's face flashes across the screen with their own line, and your element hits every enemy on screen (solar flares / a black hole / chain lightning).
- Then the ship transforms, grows, glows with its element, every weapon is at full power with the beam always on, nearby enemy bullets melt away, and it ends with a blast.

Feel every hit
- Enemies flash white and show the chunk of health you just took; bigger enemies show a health ring when hit; damaged enemies crack, spark and smoke; the boss bar has phase marks.
- Level-up is a moment now (POWER 2/3/4 and the new weapon on show); stronger-looking early weapons; close grazes charge your Super.
- Obstacles look real (soot, snow squalls, blast doors), move more in later missions, and each sector's monsters break apart in their own material.

v0.5 0.5+9595356 · 2026-09-30 04:01 UTC

▶ Play in browser · ⬇ Android APK · iPhone: TestFlight

Nova Strike v0.5: six real sectors, three real arsenals, raid bosses, a much harder campaign.

Weapons (all three aircraft, every mission)
- Solar (Striker): the EXP-004 arsenal everywhere: front cannons, Sunseeker rockets that curve onto enemies (3x bigger at full power), molten shells that arc and burst, the core laser.
- Void (Guardian): heavy void shells, gravity torpedoes that pull enemies and enemy bullets in then implode, Bastion shield nodes that eat bullets and fire them back, a slow dark event-horizon beam.
- Arc (Arcanist): arc packets that chain between enemies, ion seekers that mark targets, relay drones holding a lightning fence, a forking storm lance.
- Every weapon grows with Power (1-4) and its Hangar tier (Mk I-IV), and you can always see what is hitting.
- New pilots start with every weapon at Mk I; existing saves keep their upgrades.

Six sectors, each with its own monsters, enemy bullets and obstacles
- Lantern Road: Wick monsters that drink lantern light; Blackout bands and lanterns you relight to burn enemy bullets.
- Emberwake Foundries: heat and hammer machines; slag rivers and the Firestorm: the whole map burns except a small circle you must stay inside (still, then moving, then two in a row).
- Tempest Expanse: linked storm units; electric corridors, static build-up if you stand still, white-out blizzard bands.
- Hollow Veil: monsters that remember your moves; folds that swap the lane's halves, walls built from your own path.
- Crownfall Armada: fleet formations with rails and flak; broadsides and closing hangar doors.
- Final Dominion: the harvest choir; every earlier twist returns and the core drains the light.
- Obstacles teach first, then test, then twist, and every one leaves a way out.

Bosses
- All six are raid fights: sealed cores, adds that refill or heal, safe zones, core windows and an enrage timer, each from its story (Algol's Eclipse, the Sovereign's Firestorm, the Seraph's cage, the Oracle's replayed path, boarding the Regent's Spear, silencing Dominion Prime's song).

Harder
- Enemy waves stay until you destroy them; every mission is harder than the one before; the campaign expects Hangar upgrades.
- Enemy bullets and beams are bigger and fully rendered (hot core, body, trail, impact) and keep their red core so danger always reads.

Menus
- Home: CAMPAIGN and ENDLESS replace PLAY.
- Campaign opens the universe of the six sectors along the Lantern Road; tap a sector for its 8 missions.

v0.4 0.4+0a00b06 · 2026-09-29 09:04 UTC

▶ Play in browser · ⬇ Android APK · iPhone: TestFlight

Nova Strike v0.4: finer finish, stronger combat.

Look
- Enemies repainted: solid gunmetal hulls with crimson only on engines and weapons; they bank into turns, fire from their guns and break apart by size.
- Your aircraft: clean graphite, titanium and gold with no patchy highlights; the same finish in Home, Hangar and combat.
- Shots redesigned: shorter Solar lances with a clear rhythm, forked Arc bolts, pulsing Void shells; enemy needles, teardrops, heavy bolts, seekers and beams stay readable over explosions.
- Hits flash where they land; heavy enemies crack before they explode.
- Sharper Home background; calmer highlights behind the fight.

Combat
- Weapon levels feel different: L2 adds side rhythm, L3 a heavier accent, L4 a signature volley for each aircraft.
- Lose a weapon level and a short-lived recovery core may appear in a safe spot.
- Solar Breaker: two blades converge on a target. Arc Relay: hunts and chains in order. Guardian: absorbed shots charge facets and fire back.
- Supers transform the aircraft: Solar wings, Void armour plates, Arc spires.
- Enemy squads: a support unit shields or boosts a partner; destroy it and the squad falls apart.
- Each destination has its own set piece: station gate, heat vents, storm corridor, gravity field, capital-ship passage and a final gauntlet.
- Boss parts matter: destroy a turret, sensor or shield pylon and that attack is gone.

Menus and feel
- Home shows three clear strengths; the Hangar groups upgrades as Firepower, Survival and Systems, with a free firing preview before you buy.
- First flight moves on as soon as you've learned each step.
- New audio mix and vibration feedback (can be turned off in Settings).
- Full-quality cutscenes, backgrounds and portraits now really download in the background (they didn't in v0.3).
- Arabic power level shows "م1–م4".

Performance (real phones, Test Lab, mission 1.08 with its boss)
- Pixel 10 Pro XL 59 fps (high quality), Pixel 9 and 10a 59 fps, Galaxy S25 / S25 Ultra 118 fps, S24 90 fps, A56 79 fps.
- Budget phones run 15–41 fps (for example Galaxy A16 22 fps, moto g 2025 41 fps): below 60.

Known limits
- Supers, set pieces and dense volleys were not timed separately on phones; the obstacle planner's browser timing is not measured yet.
- The final sound mix was measured, not yet checked by ear on a phone speaker.
- Studio test tools (MORE → Studio test) are on in tester builds.

Nova Strike v0.3: sharper look, livelier combat, simpler menus.

Look
- Sharper on high-end phones: the aircraft render at full resolution, with warm key light on the gold trim and cool edges on the graphite.
- Tighter glow, crisper shots with thin dark outlines, calmer highlights behind the fight.
- Enemies are now dark burgundy and gunmetal with crimson lights; only you and your rewards are gold.
- Full-quality cutscenes, backgrounds and portraits download in the background after the first launch (Settings shows the progress).

Combat
- Three enemy attack styles: scouts fire short aimed bursts, assault ships strafe with fan volleys, heavies charge a narrow beam, then pause.
- Enemy fire has new shapes: needles, teardrops, heavy bolts and beams, in coral and raspberry.
- Obstacles: tumbling wreckage and station gates, always announced and always with a way through.
- Solar Breaker replaces Overdrive Dash: a crescent of solar fire that cuts through enemies.
- Arc Relay now strikes the enemies and chains between them.
- Guardian's shield absorbs hits, then releases a shockwave.
- Stronger Super transformations with their own sounds.
- New pickup icons, each with its own sound.

Menus
- Home has three buttons: PLAY, UPGRADE and MORE.
- Installing an upgrade shows the part being built onto your aircraft.
- Upgrade tiers open as you clear the campaign (tier II after Stage 1, III after Stage 3, IV after Stage 5). Tiers you already own stay yours.
- Bigger, bolder text everywhere, in English and Arabic.
- First flight: a short, safe opening that teaches steering, pickups and your special.

Nova Strike v0.2: the big look pass.

New

Fixed

Gameplay
