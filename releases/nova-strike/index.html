Nova Strike v0.9: your aircraft are the collection ships, the whole game restyled, one Journey map.

Your aircraft
- Striker, Guardian and Arcanist are now the ships from the aircraft collection: gold, chrome and gems. Every Power level bolts real parts onto the ship and escort drones lock in beside it; the Super shows the Super form.
- Each aircraft has the collection's four premium finishes (names in English and Arabic). Old skins carry over by look; Supporters keep their gold finish; nobody loses a skin.

Upgrades
- Three clear paths: Weapons, Hull & Shield, Special Systems. Each card shows what the next buy gives you. Every few buys your ship visibly changes (new abilities like Overcharge, Deflect, Last Light, Crown).
- Your old upgrades are moved over automatically; no credits are lost.

Enemies and attacks
- Enemies are polished chrome and obsidian with ruby cores; bosses restyled part by part, with new intro and death scenes.
- Lasers warn, grow, burn exactly where they are drawn, and fade. Shots always leave a gun or eye on the enemy's body. Every wave is checked so there is always a way out.
- Obstacles and walls match their sector; their edges hit exactly where they are drawn. Six obstacles can be shot apart.

Campaign
- One tall Journey map up through the six sectors. It opens where you are; tap a place to see the mission and PLAY. Beating a sector's boss lifts the haze over it.

Menus and sound
- Simpler menus: one clear thing to do on every screen; Arabic checked on every screen.
- Fuller, comfortable sound: crisp rapid fire, weighty Supers, warnings you always hear above the music.

Honest status (studio art bar: 4.5 of 5 next to the paintings, in motion)
- Met: your aircraft (4.5).
- Not yet met, carried to the next version: weapon effects (about 3.5-3.75), bosses (3.3-4.2), enemies (3.6), obstacles (3.0), props (3.6), the Journey map (3.8 English / 3.7 Arabic).
- Not run this round: the outside "name what you see" checks for aircraft levels, obstacles and Journey markers.
- The start-up screen still shows the old Striker picture; new key art comes next version.

v0.8 0.8+0436c0a · 2026-10-02 00:02 UTC

▶ Play in browser · ⬇ Android APK · iPhone: TestFlight

Nova Strike v0.8: the Super is back, weapons you can see, smoother play.

Super
- The Super stops time again: about 1.7 s of deep slow motion, the pilot slides in at the top of the screen, says their line, and the aircraft transforms. Nothing can hit you while the pilot is on screen, and the middle of the screen (where threats are) stays clear.
- Supers come more often: about 3-4 per mission.

Weapons
- Every Power level adds one new weapon you can see. Striker: twin needles, smoke-trail rockets, crossing slipstream darts, a flame lance. Guardian: black marbles, swirl wells, stacking facets, a black star. Arcanist: zigzag hops, mark sigils, a lightning fence, a branching lance.
- Shots are small and crisp again; no giant yellow column.

Look
- The first enemies glow crimson and mean again, with clean edges.
- The fight screen is brighter: lantern lights along the road, a key light on your aircraft. (Measured: lightness 26.9, up from 20.3, target 26.5 met; the dark part of the screen is 55 %, down from 64 % but above the 50 % aim, accepted by the studio lead for this version.)
- VICTORY is a reward again: a big pilot portrait, a brighter screen.

Pilots
- Each pilot now has many lines per moment (up to 10, 18 for bosses), in English and Saudi Arabic. You won't hear the same line twice in a mission; only the Super line repeats.

Challenge
- Later sectors bite again if you stop dodging; bosses tuned so every aircraft can win with the upgrades you own by then.

Smoother play
- Everything a mission needs is loaded before it starts, and effects are prepared on the loading screen, so missions no longer stutter when a new effect appears. The first launch after installing prepares your aircraft once (about 50 s, during the HD download); in the first missions after installing, 2-3 short stutters per mission can remain (worst about a quarter of a second, measured on a Pixel 10 Pro XL), and none after that (0 on the second launch).
- The HD art downloads at start-up, before you play. On the web it is saved in your browser, so it doesn't download again on your next visit (tested in Chrome and Edge; Firefox and Android Chrome not tested yet). The browser may still clear it if your device runs short of space; then it downloads once more.

Nova Strike v0.7: sharper, deliberate, rewarding.

Look
- The three aircraft show their graphite panels, titanium edges, gold trim and engines instead of dark silhouettes, in Home, Hangar and flight.
- All 30 enemies re-finished: real armour planes, a thin clean edge instead of the old glow, coral lenses and engines.
- Enemy fire is always coral/crimson with a dark outline and is never hidden by your weapons.
- Foreground parts (lanterns, gates, vents, doors, pylons, boss hammer and cover) have real metal, depth and light.

Combat
- Enemies attack in turns now: a warning, one clear attack, a moment to recover. No more bullets from everywhere at once.
- Each aircraft changes visibly at every Power step: Striker's rockets, molten shells and fire beam; Guardian's pulling torpedoes and protective emitters; Arcanist's ordered lightning jumps and forking lance.
- Power climbs in four steps you can feel: Power 2 at about 15 s, Power 3 at about 35 s, Power 4 near the end. A hull hit costs one step; a recovery core brings it back.
- Solar Crescent, Returnfire Bastion and Hunter Relay reworked; the three Supers transform the aircraft, and nothing can hit you while the pilot cut-in covers the screen.
- CLEAN SWEEP: clear a marked group quickly without being hit for a small bonus.
- Warnings are short and point at the danger; after you've seen one twice it becomes an icon.

Bosses
- All six bosses reworked to teach one rule at a time; no hidden damage timers. Every boss can be beaten with upgrades you can actually own by then.

Sound
- 160 new layered sound effects: every weapon, hit, explosion and pickup; warnings stay audible over full-power fire.

Upgrades
- The Hangar now has three choices: WEAPONS, ARMOUR, CORE (Rank I-IV). Where an old save continues (the web version), every upgrade you bought is kept and converted.
- TRY NEXT: an 8-second preview of the next rank before you buy.

Performance
- Smooth on high-end phones (Pixel 10 Pro XL ~59 FPS). Budget phones still run below 60 FPS in busy fights (Galaxy A16 ~23, A05s ~23-25, moto g ~40-42, A56 ~59 FPS on our test phones); the slowdown seen earlier in this version is fixed on the A16 and moto g. Work continues.

Also
- Browser HD graphics download fixed, with progress and a Retry button in Settings.
- Fixed enemies showing as white squares on Medium/Low graphics (and on Auto on slower devices).
- The studio is now Kari & Bros Studio.
- New Reduced motion setting.

Nova Strike v0.6: unique bosses, a real difficulty wall, arcade warnings, a true Super moment, health you can feel.

Bosses: each one has its own mechanic now
- Algol (Lantern Road): twin engines orbiting each other. Only the twin in the light can be hurt; every eclipse swaps them. Enrage: total eclipse.
- Furnace Sovereign (Emberwake): forges a giant hammer during the fight. Break the glowing rivets before it's done, or it slams the half of the screen you're in. Enrage: meltdown, a hammer every few seconds.
- Storm Seraph (Tempest): marks you as its lightning rod. Fly next to its own drones and the lightning hits them instead ("REDIRECTED!"). Enrage: the whole sky strikes.
- Null Oracle (Hollow Veil): a sweeping vision cone. Outside it you're safe but can't hurt it; inside you can, but it fires straight at you. Later: two cones and a false one. Enrage: the eye opens wide and closes on you.
- Regent's Spear (Crownfall): locks on over 3 seconds. Hide behind its own wreckage and drones to break the lock and open its weak point. Enrage: the whole fleet locks on.
- Dominion Prime (Final Dominion): THE HARVEST steals your rockets, then shells, then laser; break its crown vessels to take them back, then finish it with your full arsenal.
- Every phase is faster than the last, late phases throw two attacks at once, and every enrage is a real wall.

Much harder, and upgrades matter
- Enemy damage now grows every sector (up to 3.5x in the last sector), bosses hit harder, and the long safety blink after a hit is gone.
- Without Hangar upgrades you can't beat the later bosses; upgraded and dodging, you can. Defense upgrades are the answer to damage.
- Story mode stays gentler; Veteran is harder.

Arcade warnings
- Big full-width warning bands with hazard stripes, huge titles, a siren and flashing screen edges before every obstacle and boss attack, in English and Arabic, each sector with its own style. After the first two times, a quick icon at the danger spot instead.

The Super moment
- Pressing Super slows time, your pilot's face flashes across the screen with their own line, and your element hits every enemy on screen (solar flares / a black hole / chain lightning).
- Then the ship transforms, grows, glows with its element, every weapon is at full power with the beam always on, nearby enemy bullets melt away, and it ends with a blast.

Feel every hit
- Enemies flash white and show the chunk of health you just took; bigger enemies show a health ring when hit; damaged enemies crack, spark and smoke; the boss bar has phase marks.
- Level-up is a moment now (POWER 2/3/4 and the new weapon on show); stronger-looking early weapons; close grazes charge your Super.
- Obstacles look real (soot, snow squalls, blast doors), move more in later missions, and each sector's monsters break apart in their own material.

Nova Strike v0.5: six real sectors, three real arsenals, raid bosses, a much harder campaign.

Weapons (all three aircraft, every mission)
- Solar (Striker): the EXP-004 arsenal everywhere: front cannons, Sunseeker rockets that curve onto enemies (3x bigger at full power), molten shells that arc and burst, the core laser.
- Void (Guardian): heavy void shells, gravity torpedoes that pull enemies and enemy bullets in then implode, Bastion shield nodes that eat bullets and fire them back, a slow dark event-horizon beam.
- Arc (Arcanist): arc packets that chain between enemies, ion seekers that mark targets, relay drones holding a lightning fence, a forking storm lance.
- Every weapon grows with Power (1-4) and its Hangar tier (Mk I-IV), and you can always see what is hitting.
- New pilots start with every weapon at Mk I; existing saves keep their upgrades.

Six sectors, each with its own monsters, enemy bullets and obstacles
- Lantern Road: Wick monsters that drink lantern light; Blackout bands and lanterns you relight to burn enemy bullets.
- Emberwake Foundries: heat and hammer machines; slag rivers and the Firestorm: the whole map burns except a small circle you must stay inside (still, then moving, then two in a row).
- Tempest Expanse: linked storm units; electric corridors, static build-up if you stand still, white-out blizzard bands.
- Hollow Veil: monsters that remember your moves; folds that swap the lane's halves, walls built from your own path.
- Crownfall Armada: fleet formations with rails and flak; broadsides and closing hangar doors.
- Final Dominion: the harvest choir; every earlier twist returns and the core drains the light.
- Obstacles teach first, then test, then twist, and every one leaves a way out.

Bosses
- All six are raid fights: sealed cores, adds that refill or heal, safe zones, core windows and an enrage timer, each from its story (Algol's Eclipse, the Sovereign's Firestorm, the Seraph's cage, the Oracle's replayed path, boarding the Regent's Spear, silencing Dominion Prime's song).

Harder
- Enemy waves stay until you destroy them; every mission is harder than the one before; the campaign expects Hangar upgrades.
- Enemy bullets and beams are bigger and fully rendered (hot core, body, trail, impact) and keep their red core so danger always reads.

Menus
- Home: CAMPAIGN and ENDLESS replace PLAY.
- Campaign opens the universe of the six sectors along the Lantern Road; tap a sector for its 8 missions.
