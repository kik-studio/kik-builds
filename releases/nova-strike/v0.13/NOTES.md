# Nova Strike v0.13: what changed

- Full-quality art: every texture in the game is back to its original quality (no compression); the tester download is bigger because of it.
- Bosses break under fire: armour parts crack and tear off while you shoot them, and every boss and raid boss now ends with a big finale.
- Heavier hits: stronger shots, a short freeze on heavy kills, and a sound per power level.
- Light shards: shot-down enemies drop light shards; fly through them in a row to fill the Super faster.
- Enemy bullets stay readable over your beam; in Sectors 1 to 4 the sides show more of the painted scenery while the middle lane stays calm.
- Wingtip rocket pods on the Striker, and the Super form is now clearly the biggest ship.
- The midpoint elite of each sector switches between attacks (three in Sector 1), can flee, and comes back changed.
- Sector 1 starts faster, boss tips sit in one line away from your ship, banners stay on top of the action, and the Algol twin-boss fight has a steadier length.
