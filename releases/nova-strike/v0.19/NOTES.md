# Nova Strike v0.19

- All nine bosses have new, more detailed 3D models: Algol, Furnace Sovereign, Storm Seraph, Null Oracle, Regent's Spear, Dominion Prime, and the raid bosses Husk-Wright, Thuban and the Archive of Orders. Their guns, pods and glowing parts are now made from the same models.
- Thuban the dragon has a brand-new head, with a jaw that opens when it attacks.
- Boss pictures on the journey map, briefings and raid cards now show the real 3D bosses.
- The HD content download now resumes by itself if the connection drops or the server hiccups.
- Fairer elite fights: when a sector elite dies, its escorts stop their attacks at once, so nothing hits you right after the kill.
- Smoother play: a long freeze when some bosses arrived is fixed, and phones on lower graphics settings use a lighter boss model.
- Smoothness in the big boss fights on mid-range phones (Galaxy A54 class) is being improved.
