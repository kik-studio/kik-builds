# Nova Strike v0.23

- Every sector's enemies now fly in groups that enter, hold, attack and leave, and each sector has its own obstacles and bullet shapes, so you can read what is coming.
- Enemies look like machines when hit, impacts feel heavier, and the Solar shots are shaped flames; the Bomb and tactical abilities show clearly where they hit.
- Bosses show each new phase with their own crack, glow and attack.
- Pickups stand out, the background steps back behind the fight, and Super and warning cues no longer cover the play area.
- Sounds land on the exact moment: the Bomb sound now plays when its wave goes out, and music changes smoothly between parts of a mission.
- New Home, Campaign, Briefing and Hangar screens: the Hangar shows exactly what each upgrade gives, with a ship preview at Power 4.
- After a mission the result screen tells you what you gained, what ended the run, and one next step from your own run (for example a missed power core or an unused Bomb).
- Mission 5.06 is back to its intended challenge, and power-ups no longer freeze the game for a moment.
- Fixed: the final boss's weapon-steal warnings show the weapon's name in full; Hangar weapon texts match the real weapons, the pause screen's leave button fits in every language, and the result screen fits small phones.
