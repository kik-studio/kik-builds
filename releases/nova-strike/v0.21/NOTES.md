# Nova Strike v0.21

- Your pilot account: your progress is saved to it, and a move code in Settings brings it to a new phone. After a mission you can add your call sign to the squadron roster (players who already fly see this once; "Later" skips it).
- Settings has a new account card: your call sign, the move code, and delete account.
- In Arabic the squadron now speaks to you as طيّار or طيّارة: pick it once at the start (Later keeps طيّار), change it any time in Settings. Pilots' voice lines follow your choice too.
- Boss arrivals are smoother: Storm Seraph, Regent's Spear, Thuban, Algol and the Furnace Sovereign arrive without the short freeze they had (measured on PC).
- Big boss fights draw far fewer pieces per frame (health rings, the Null Oracle's cones and damaged enemies). Their smoothness on mid-range phones (Galaxy A54 class) is measured on a real phone right after this update.
- The Foundry Overseer no longer raises its arms for nothing: when it cannot pour, it shows the warning of the attack it really uses.
- Enemy volleys can no longer close the only way through a gate.
- Shard chains now count game time, so a pause or a short stutter no longer breaks a chain.
- The mission briefing fits short phones in Arabic, even with the longest boss names.
