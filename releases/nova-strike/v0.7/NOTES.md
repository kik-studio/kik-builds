Nova Strike v0.7: sharper, deliberate, rewarding.

Look
- The three aircraft show their graphite panels, titanium edges, gold trim and engines instead of dark silhouettes, in Home, Hangar and flight.
- All 30 enemies re-finished: real armour planes, a thin clean edge instead of the old glow, coral lenses and engines.
- Enemy fire is always coral/crimson with a dark outline and is never hidden by your weapons.
- Foreground parts (lanterns, gates, vents, doors, pylons, boss hammer and cover) have real metal, depth and light.

Combat
- Enemies attack in turns now: a warning, one clear attack, a moment to recover. No more bullets from everywhere at once.
- Each aircraft changes visibly at every Power step: Striker's rockets, molten shells and fire beam; Guardian's pulling torpedoes and protective emitters; Arcanist's ordered lightning jumps and forking lance.
- Power climbs in four steps you can feel: Power 2 at about 15 s, Power 3 at about 35 s, Power 4 near the end. A hull hit costs one step; a recovery core brings it back.
- Solar Crescent, Returnfire Bastion and Hunter Relay reworked; the three Supers transform the aircraft, and nothing can hit you while the pilot cut-in covers the screen.
- CLEAN SWEEP: clear a marked group quickly without being hit for a small bonus.
- Warnings are short and point at the danger; after you've seen one twice it becomes an icon.

Bosses
- All six bosses reworked to teach one rule at a time; no hidden damage timers. Every boss can be beaten with upgrades you can actually own by then.

Sound
- 160 new layered sound effects: every weapon, hit, explosion and pickup; warnings stay audible over full-power fire.

Upgrades
- The Hangar now has three choices: WEAPONS, ARMOUR, CORE (Rank I-IV). Where an old save continues (the web version), every upgrade you bought is kept and converted.
- TRY NEXT: an 8-second preview of the next rank before you buy.

Performance
- Smooth on high-end phones (Pixel 10 Pro XL ~59 FPS). Budget phones still run below 60 FPS in busy fights (Galaxy A16 ~23, A05s ~23-25, moto g ~40-42, A56 ~59 FPS on our test phones); the slowdown seen earlier in this version is fixed on the A16 and moto g. Work continues.

Also
- Browser HD graphics download fixed, with progress and a Retry button in Settings.
- Fixed enemies showing as white squares on Medium/Low graphics (and on Auto on slower devices).
- The studio is now Kari & Bros Studio.
- New Reduced motion setting.
