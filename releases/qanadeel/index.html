qanadeel: versions for review

v0.4 0.4+6a2c93e · 2026-11-01 18:43 UTC

⬇ Android APK · iPhone: TestFlight

- New: Pearl Lagoon, the sixth game. Drag up from the shell to aim (the dots show the path, even off a wall), let go to shoot. Three or more pearls of a colour pop, and pearls left hanging fall free and thread onto a necklace along the shore. 60 levels and a daily board. Out of shots? Retry for free or undo your last shot.
- All six games are now playable from the home, and the daily puzzle rotates between all six (one daily reward per day).
- A lighter app on Android: the download is now about 150 MB instead of about 205 MB, with nothing made smaller. Light Trails chapters 2 to 6 download by themselves after the first start on Android (the iPhone app carries them inside); every game plays meanwhile, and a chapter still on its way shows a small download ring.
- Light Trails now uses the same hint bar as the other games, and its won coins fly to your wallet at the top.
- In this test version the video hint button gives the hint at once (no video plays). If a hint does not work, nothing is spent.
- Pearl Lagoon hints aim the shell for you: tap to shoot along the dots. Some hints first take a few shots back, for free.
- Tall phones: the painting behind every game now reaches the top of the screen.
- Moon Rings: a locked ring now shakes clearly, and the guiding hand follows the ring's curve.
- Small fixes: bigger coin and hint icons in the shop, a dark backing behind the Pocket Exit hint line, a shorter Arabic hint line in Gem Flare, and the first achievement message no longer covers the Light Trails guide line.
- New sounds and music for Pearl Lagoon.

v0.3 0.3+c54d8eb · 2026-11-01 11:17 UTC

⬇ Android APK · iPhone: TestFlight

- Two new games in the collection: five of the six games are now playable from the home.
- New: Moon Rings. Turn the stone rings until the moon's light can flow from the pearl to the gate; the water runs on its own as soon as the way is open. 60 levels and a daily board.
- New: Gem Flare. Tap groups of three or more matching gems to collect the colors the board asks for, within the moves you have. 60 levels and a daily board. Out of moves? Retry for free or undo your last move.
- The daily puzzle now rotates between all five games, and you can earn one daily reward per day.
- One hint bar in Color Dew, Pocket Exit, Moon Rings and Gem Flare: use a stored hint or spend coins. If a hint does not work, nothing is spent. The video hint is not on yet in this test version: its button says "no video right now".
- Pocket Exit hints now always help, even on the hardest boards ("This hint goes back a few moves, then plays the right one.").
- A guiding hand shows the first moves on levels 1 to 3 of Color Dew and Pocket Exit.
- Pocket Exit levels now get harder step by step inside each chapter (19 of the 60 levels changed places; your progress is kept).
- New sounds and music for Moon Rings and Gem Flare.
- Won coins in Color Dew, Pocket Exit, Moon Rings and Gem Flare now fly to your wallet at the top.
- The home's collection shows how many levels you finished in each game.
- Color Dew keeps its mix of tall and short tubes.
- Small fixes: the opening painting fills the screen, a smoother lantern edge in Light Trails, the right coin and hint icons in the shop, and Pocket Exit's floor stones line up with the parking spaces.

Still being worked on (next versions):
- Moon Rings: the shake of a locked ring is small on a phone screen.
- Very tall phones: a plain dark band shows above the world painting.
- Light Trails: won coins fly to the win card, not yet to the wallet at the top like the other four games.
- Shop: the coin and hint icons look small next to their numbers.
- Pocket Exit: the Arabic rewind hint line is hard to read where it crosses the bright pier lamps.
- Gem Flare: the Arabic rewind hint line wraps to two lines at normal text size.

v0.2 0.2+0ab0235 · 2026-10-31 18:19 UTC

⬇ Android APK · iPhone: TestFlight

- Qanadeel is now a collection: a new home with six puzzle games, three to play now and three coming soon.
- Light Trails (the light-path game you know): all 300 levels and your progress, coins and hints kept, with a new carved-stone board and glowing gems. Each level you solve still lights a lantern in the chapter's painting.

- Every colored piece carries a symbol, and reduce motion turns the celebrations into simple fades.
- Please try on your phone: type your name in Arabic with the keyboard, drag very fast, and touch the board with two fingers at once (only one move should happen).
- Qanadeel has its new logo and app icon.

v0.1 0.1+70c4512 · 2026-10-30 17:26 UTC

⬇ Android APK · iPhone: TestFlight

- First test version of Qanadeel: join matching lights and fill every tile to light a lantern.
- 300 levels in 6 chapters; every level you solve lights one lantern in the chapter's painting.
- A daily puzzle every day, playable offline.
- The first three levels teach the game with a moving finger, no long texts.
- Every chapter has its own calm music, and each finished pair plays a note that builds into a chord.
- Finishing a chapter wakes its whole painting before the next one opens.
- No lives, no timers: undo and restart at any time; draw by tapping instead of dragging from Settings > Accessibility.
- Hints show the next path; coins come from solving boards, daily puzzles and gifts.
- Arabic and English; pick your name and play.
- Shop, themes and achievements are in. The shop shows its items and prices, but buying is switched off in this test; hint videos are not available yet.
- The logo is plain text for now. Android first; iPhone comes after 1 November.
