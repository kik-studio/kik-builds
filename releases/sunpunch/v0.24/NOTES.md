- Fixed: on smaller phones the level-up banner no longer covers the "drag to move" tip; it now appears just below it.
- Levels 11, 17 and 18 are hard again (they had become a little easier than in 0.22).

Honest note on the art: the same as v0.23 (the power buttons, warnings and new obstacles read better but are still below our own look bar; the Big Bite boss will be remade so it looks and feels better).
