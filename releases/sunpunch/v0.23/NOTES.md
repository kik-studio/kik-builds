# SUNPUNCH v0.23: what changed

- The Super lasts much longer now (6 to 7.5 seconds, longer for stronger aircraft families) and ends with one big closing volley.
- The power buttons have no words any more: a clenching sun-fist and your aircraft family's own wings show charging, ready, active and cooldown.
- An arcade announcer calls your powers ("SUNPUNCH!", each family's Super call, "RAGE!"); you can switch the voice off in Settings.
- Harder levels: obstacles and enemies now come together, with up to 4 obstacle types per level (2 on Level 1, 3 on Level 2).
- One clean warning style: a lit lane or footprint shows where danger comes and fills up until it arrives. Warnings that touch now draw as one shape instead of piling on each other.
- RAGE: if a fight drags on, the enemies get angry, attack nonstop and finally dive at you one by one, and the level ends within about 3 minutes; you can't hide in one spot forever. In normal play you will rarely meet it (about 1 run in 25).
- Brood Tides no longer hold a level up: when the wave is beaten the tide winds down and swims away, so Levels 2 and 3 keep moving.
- Enemies and obstacles crowd each other less: fewer ships sit on top of each other on Levels 6 and 10.
- Bigger enemies now get to show their attack before they fall, and early enemies are tougher and attack sooner.
- Restored full-quality art from earlier versions (aircraft paint, boss textures, flight sprites).

Honest note: the new warnings and power buttons read better than before but are still below our own look bar; busy moments are not yet as clean as we want, obstacles and enemies don't mix as much as we want on every level, and small enemies (razors, puffs, claws) still often fall before they attack. Level 9 can still turn to RAGE in about one run in five. The Home screen is being checked again on a real phone. We keep working on all of these.

Honest note on the art: the new power buttons and warnings read better than before but are still below our own 4.5 look bar, and the obstacles of the new levels are too (new obstacle art, stones and menus improved, still below the bar). The full-quality art of earlier versions is restored; the 3-D Big Bite body stays switched off for now. We keep improving all of it.
