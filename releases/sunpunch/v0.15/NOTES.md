# SUNPUNCH v0.15: what changed (patch)

- Intro cards now show the creature's picture, its name and a 2-4 word instruction for 2.5 s, with a pulsing outline on the real enemy (EN + AR, Arabic reviewed).
- Breakable things crack in orange as you hit them; deadly round parts get a jagged pulsing outline (no more target rings that look like "shoot here").
- Defeat card headline is "SHOT DOWN" over "Coins kept"; the CONTINUE label no longer wraps letter by letter.
- The Vault stone on the map has a one-line label (tap 24 times in 6 s).
- The "DRAG TO MOVE" banner no longer covers the "Collect stars" hint.
- Fixed a Coil attack (lamp rivet volley) that did not fire because of a script error.
- Review captures now show the v0.14 screens; the phone guard slot is 7 minutes.
