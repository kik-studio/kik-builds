# SUNPUNCH v0.25: what changed

## New
- Big Bite (Level 10) is remade as a real 3D shark: its jaw opens at the natural joint, it dives through the field, and it has new attacks (a lunge pair, dive and surface, a triple bite with Gunlets, a 5-bite last stand). It has the same health as before but hits much harder: a good player takes several times more hits than in 0.24.
- Coil the dragon (Level 20) is one big 3D dragon: the holes burst and merge into one, the dragon climbs out and fills the screen, and it rears up and breathes across the screen when it gets angry.
- Both bosses have their own new music theme and new sounds (arrival, attacks, dives, hits, defeat).
- Sector 2 weapons look like a welding war (heat beams, sparks, glowing rivets) and are harder; Sector 1 levels 3-10 are a step harder.
- Most enemies (over 9 in 10) show their attack before they fall, none leaves the screen without a fight, and obstacle walls almost never come without enemies.
- Level 1 opens with a row of star shells to break.
- A new Store, a smaller but richer launch sheet, a Home button on the Map that looks like a button, and new power buttons. The power tab is gone.
- The upgrade moment on Home keeps its burst going and flashes, in English and Arabic.
- The crane obstacle shows what it will do before it swings; warnings are cleaner.
- The art is back at full quality (the app is about 360 MB).

## Fixed
- The RAGE hunter hurt you from anywhere on screen; now only when it touches you.
- Big Bite's Gunlets could hide inside its jaw.
- Coil played Big Bite's music; it now has its own.
- A slowdown on Home after visiting the hangar (memory use cut by more than half).

## Honest notes
- Not checked on a real phone this version (smoothness and phone start-up): tell us if anything stutters or will not start.
- Sector 2 is clearly harder than in 0.24 (our test pilots clear about 4 in 10 runs instead of 6 in 10), and Level 18 is the hardest: our test pilots did not clear it at all. Tell us if it feels unfair.
- Coil (Level 20) is not harder than in 0.24 for a skilled player; the harder boss work went into Big Bite this time.
- The optional download pack for extra art is not ready yet, so all the art is inside the app (nothing was cut to make it smaller).
- The game stays at 20 levels and 2 bosses this version: Sectors 3 and 4 and their bosses (Kiln Mantis, Mother Lure) are not coming.
