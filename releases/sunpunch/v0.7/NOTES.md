SUNPUNCH v0.7: the whole of Midnight Reef, four aircraft worth wanting, a living Big Bite (internal candidate)
- **Ten levels instead of one:** Midnight Reef is now a full sector of ten levels, from the warm entrance to Big Bite's Maw. Each level adds one new idea (Puff fans, breakable walls, Claw rhythm, spires, formations...) and teaches it on its own first. Level 1 is open; each clear opens the next, and every cleared level can be flown again.
- **A real campaign map:** scroll up a painted chain of floating islands. Your current level glows orange, a tapped level gets a light ring, and the card shows the level, its length and your best. Three more sectors (Ember Shoals, Wreck Field, The Drop-off) wait above the clouds, clearly locked and "coming in a future update".
- **Aircraft you want next:** the hangar shows the next aircraft in the spotlight while Juno builds it on her workbench as your coins come in. Try it in a short test flight before you buy. Buying plays a short reveal (parts lock in, engines ignite, YOURS), which you can skip.
- **Every aircraft grows four times in a flight:** Spark, Striker, Phoenix and Helios each have their own look and weapons at every FLIGHT LV, and their own Daystar transformation. Striker clears the same fight about 16-18 % faster than Spark.
- **Big Bite is alive:** it watches you, opens its jaw to show the core, transforms into Inferno at half health, and breaks apart with a burst of coins.
- **Enemies with character:** Razors bank and aim before they fire, Puffs swell before a fan, Claws swing left and right; each breaks apart in its own way.
- **A new flight screen:** a segmented HEALTH bar, a FLIGHT LV bar with MAX, a slim tracker showing how far you are through the level (Big Bite's icon only in Level 10), and new Sunpunch and Daystar buttons.
- **Rewards for ten levels:** later levels pay more, every level has a one-time first-clear bonus, and finishing the sector pays a bonus. Helios now costs 1,100 coins.
- **Menus redone:** home, pause, settings, level clear, sector complete and defeat cards match the new style, in English and Arabic. Clearing a level now opens its own LEVEL CLEAR card, and beating Big Bite opens SECTOR COMPLETE.
- **A darker, clearer battlefield:** a painted cloud channel runs behind your lane, so shots, coins and enemies stand out.
- **Still to come (honest):** the Sunpunch blast is close but not yet at our quality bar; Spark, Phoenix and Helios share one level-up sound (Striker has its own); on our test phones (Galaxy A55/A56, Pixel 9/10) the game holds 60 fps or more, with one short hiccup left when Big Bite turns into Inferno on the Galaxy phones; iPhone not measured yet. Level 1 can feel empty near its end if you clear it fast (we tune that next). This is an internal candidate: no outside players have tried it yet.
- **Art spend (internal):** Sunpunch sprites 96 and the lane cloud 80 Scenario credits on our own style model; the boss wreck and the parked Spark were made with OpenAI (2 low drafts + 1 final each).
