# SUNPUNCH 0.27

- Your aircraft look like themselves again at home: the orange paint stays orange (no white glare), and home and flight now show the same finish.

- Zaid is bigger and always has his own place beside your aircraft (chest-up on small screens). He glances at a new pick, nods proudly when you upgrade, and cheers you back up after a loss.
- Big Bite fights with more character: its jaw loads up before each bite, its fins kick when they fire, its throat stays dark behind its bullets, and its defeat is bigger.
- Every shot now leaves from a barrel you can see on your aircraft.
- The Sector 2 enemies wind up before they shoot and recoil after, and the Remora's warning now has a sound.
- Coil's body now sits exactly where it can hit you, and the Vault's openings are drawn where you can really fly through.
- Your name now creates your account on first open; Google Play Games sign-in stays optional and can be linked later.
- The store only lets you tap a pack once its card is shown, and buttons you can't use yet stop sparkling.
