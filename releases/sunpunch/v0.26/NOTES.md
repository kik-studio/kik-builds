# SUNPUNCH 0.26

- Meet Zaid, Juno's little brother, who flies the planes she builds. He waits beside your aircraft at home, cheers when you win, nods and shares a tip when you lose, and jumps in for a split second when you hit SUNPUNCH.
- A new opening film over the reef, on first open and after each update. Tap to skip; replay it any time from Settings.
- A short welcome on first open: your name, boy or girl, and sign-in or "later". Arabic now speaks to you as a boy or a girl.
- Your own player account: pick a name and your progress is saved to your account. Sign in with Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iPhone) to keep it on a new phone, or use Settings > Account > Move my account to carry it from one phone to another. You can delete the account in Settings.
- Fairer wreck shots: a dying enemy's last shot always gives you time to dodge. Up close it bursts into a ring of shrapnel that you can slip out of. On Level 1 they are real shots that fly past you.
- The defeat screen always names what got you, with one useful tip.
- Stars now light for their own reasons: clear the level, finish with half health or more, and down every elite and carrier without RAGE. The 3-star bonus pays when all three light together.
- Leveling up mid-fight now shows on the aircraft itself, with new guns sliding out, a sun-burst and a "LEVEL UP!" call. No more banner across the screen.
- The Vault steers with the same drag as flying, chains are easier to see, the map remembers where you were, and the coin packs show coins only (still in test mode: nothing is charged).
- Delete my account now really removes everything; Start again if you change your mind.
